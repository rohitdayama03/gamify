Features

Everything your squad needs, in one app

From finding the right players to bragging about the win, Gamify covers the whole game night.

Find the right players

Search by game, country and language. Filter by rank, role and when people play.

Game profiles

Show your in-game name, rank, role, experience and achievements for each game you play.

Squads

Create a squad, invite your connections and keep the whole team chatting in one place.

Tournaments

Discover tournaments and join with a request, or host your own, post announcements and record results.

Share highlights

Post photos and videos of your best moments. Like, comment and follow what's trending in your feed.

Chat and gaming news

Message your connections, get notified about invites and tournament updates, and catch the latest gaming news.
