Privacy Policy

Gamify mobile app · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Gamify app ("Gamify", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Gamify app for Android and iOS and the servers that support it.

1. Information we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Phone numberTo create your account and sign you in with a one-time code sent by WhatsApp or SMS.
Profile details: username, bio, profile picture, country, language, games you're interested inTo show your profile to other players and help you find teammates.
Game profiles: in-game names and IDs (e.g. BGMI, eFootball, GTA), rank, role, platform, experience, achievements, availabilityTo match you with players and squads and show your stats.
Content you create: posts, photos, videos, comments, likes, chat messages, squad and tournament activityTo provide the social, chat, squad and tournament features.
Connections: players you connect with and squads you joinTo power your friends list, invites and feed.
Device push tokenTo send you notifications (messages, invites, tournament updates).
Basic technical data (IP address, request logs)To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.

We only access photos or videos that you choose to upload. We do not collect your location, contacts, or payment information, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

2. How we use your information

  • Operate your account and sign you in securely.
  • Show your profile, posts and game stats to other players.
  • Deliver messages, notifications, squad invites and tournament updates.
  • Rank and personalise your Home feed.
  • Prevent spam, fraud and abuse (for example, limiting how often sign-in codes can be requested).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. What other players can see

Your username, profile picture, bio, country, game profiles, posts and comments are visible to other Gamify users. Chat messages are visible to the people in that conversation, squad or tournament. Your phone number is not shown to other players.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers who help us run Gamify, and only as needed to do so:

  • MSG91 – sends and verifies sign-in codes (receives your phone number).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging – delivers push notifications (receives your device push token).
  • Amazon Web Services – hosts our servers, database and uploaded media.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the service.

5. Data security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Uploaded media is stored in private storage and served through short-lived links. Sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account at any time. The steps are on our account deletion page. When your account is deleted, your profile, game profiles and associated data are removed from our systems. A few limited records are kept for a short time, and that page lists them. You can also remove your profile picture or individual game profiles in the app at any time.

7. Your choices

  • Edit your profile and game details in the app.
  • Turn off notifications in your device settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by contacting us.

8. Children

Gamify is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
