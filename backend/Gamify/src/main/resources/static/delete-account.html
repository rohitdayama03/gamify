Delete your Gamify account

Gamify mobile app · developed by Rohit Dayama

You can ask us to delete your Gamify account and the data linked to it at any time. You don't need the app installed to do this.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to rohit.dayama03@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Gamify account".
  2. Include the phone number you use to sign in to Gamify, and your username if you remember it.
  3. We may reply to confirm the request comes from the owner of that phone number (for example, by asking for a code sent to it).
  4. We delete your account within 7 days of confirming the request and email you when it's done.
Email a deletion request

Data that is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is permanently removed:

  • Your account and profile: username, phone number, bio, profile picture, country, language and game interests.
  • Your game profiles (BGMI, eFootball, GTA): in-game names and IDs, rank, role, stats and achievements.
  • Your posts, including uploaded photos and videos, and your comments and likes.
  • Your chat messages and squad invites.
  • Your connections with other players.
  • Tournaments you organised, along with their announcements, and your place in tournaments you joined.
  • Your squad memberships. Squads you created pass to another member, or are deleted if no one else is in them.
  • Your device's push notification token.

We don't keep any other copy of your data after deletion. Deletion can't be undone. If you want to use Gamify again you'll need to create a new account.

Delete only some of your data

You don't have to delete your whole account. In the app you can delete individual posts and comments, remove your profile picture, remove a game profile, leave squads and tournaments, or remove connections. You can also email us to ask us to delete specific data.
