Child Safety Standards

Gamify mobile app · developed by Rohit Dayama · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Gamify has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everyone who uses Gamify and to all content shared on it, including profiles, posts, photos, videos, comments and chat messages.

1. What is prohibited

The following are strictly prohibited on Gamify:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including drawn, computer-generated or AI-generated content.
  • Grooming: befriending or communicating with a minor in order to sexually exploit or abuse them.
  • Sexualising minors in any way, including sexual comments, images or role-play involving minors.
  • Sextortion: threatening or blackmailing anyone, including minors, using intimate images.
  • Trafficking or advertising minors for sexual purposes.
  • Asking a minor to move to another app or platform for sexual purposes, or to share personal information, images or their location.
  • Any other content or behaviour that sexually exploits, abuses or endangers children.

2. Who can use Gamify

You must be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age required in your country) to use Gamify. Accounts found to belong to anyone under this age are removed.

3. How to report a concern

If you see content or behaviour on Gamify that may harm a child, report it to us right away:

  1. Email rohit.dayama03@gmail.com with the subject "Child safety report".
  2. Include the username of the account involved and a description of what you saw, such as which post, comment or chat. Please don't send us copies of abusive images. Describe them instead.

Reports are reviewed as a priority, and you can report without having a Gamify account. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services first.

4. How we respond

  • We review every child safety report promptly and remove violating content as soon as we confirm it.
  • Accounts involved in CSAE are permanently banned, and we take steps to stop them from returning.
  • We report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant national and regional authorities, including Indian law enforcement through the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in).
  • We preserve relevant information as required by law and cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.

5. Compliance with law

Gamify complies with applicable child safety laws, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, and the laws of other countries where Gamify is available.
